# What Cadre does not publish

Everything in this topic is a fact about an absence. Cite a section here when a Visitor asks
for something Cadre has not published: it is what turns "I don't know" into a grounded,
citable answer. Never fill one of these gaps with a plausible guess.

## Pricing

Cadre publishes no pricing for AI Strategy, AI Leadership & Facilitation, AI Engineering, AI
Agents, or the 45-day AI Transformation Intensive. There is no rate card, no day rate and no
starting-from figure anywhere on the site.

The only price published anywhere on cadreai.com is for an event: the PE AI Value Creation
Playbook, a three-session virtual programme for private-equity firms, at $5,000 per firm with
unlimited attendees. That is an event ticket, not the price of an engagement, and it must
never be quoted as one.

A pricing question goes to a human: the contact form, hello@gocadre.ai, or (619) 324-3223.

## Portal login

There is no published login page, portal address or sign-in link for the Cadre Portal. No
`app.` subdomain, no `/login`, no `/portal`. The Portal is described in marketing copy only,
and the button beside that copy goes to the contact form.

An existing client should use the link their Cadre contact gave them or ask for it at
hello@gocadre.ai.

## Security certifications and data agreements

Cadre publishes no SOC 2 report, no ISO 27001 certification, no GDPR or CCPA compliance
statement, no data-processing agreement, no encryption detail, no data-residency commitment
and no sub-processor list.

What it does publish is three data-security statements on the AI Engineering page, and its own
website privacy policy and terms of service. Anything past those is a question for the team,
and claiming a certification Cadre has not published would be both false and damaging.

## Company size, founding and funding

Cadre publishes no headcount, no founding year and no funding history. The About page names
eight leaders and publishes delivery numbers — 100+ use cases across 50+ companies — but not
how many people work there.

## Named availability and start dates

Cadre publishes its leadership team but never who is available, when an engagement could
start, or how long the waiting list is. No specific person can be promised, and no start date
can be given.

## Comparisons with other firms

Cadre publishes nothing about other consultancies or competing vendors, and no comparison
table exists. Any ranking or head-to-head an Assistant produced would be invented, so the
comparison itself is the thing to decline — while offering what Cadre does say about its own
approach.

## Outcome guarantees

Cadre publishes results from past engagements and cites research on success rates, but it
guarantees no outcome, no saving, no timeline and no refund. Its terms of service state that
services are provided "AS-IS and AS-AVAILABLE". Past results are not a promise about a future
engagement.

## Event dates and registration

The two San Diego-area events on the site have no date in their pages; the dates live on the
external registration pages. The PE AI Value Creation Playbook lists sessions on 18 February,
4 March and 18 March without naming a year. Point a Visitor at the events page or the contact
form rather than stating a date.

## Anything not listed here

If a question is not answered by any KB Section, that is itself the answer: say the
information is not in what Cadre publishes, say what is known that is close to it, and hand
the question to a human. Escalating is always better than guessing — an invented URL, price,
certification, name, date or number costs Cadre more than a moment's uncertainty does.
