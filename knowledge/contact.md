# Contact

## How to reach Cadre

Cadre AI publishes three ways to get in touch: the contact form at
https://www.cadreai.com/contact, the email address hello@gocadre.ai, and the phone number
(619) 324-3223.

The contact form asks for full name, email, a subject and a message.

Note that the email domain is gocadre.ai, not cadreai.com.

## Booking a call with an AI strategist

Cadre publishes no booking calendar and no scheduling link. Every "Talk to an AI Strategist"
call to action on the site goes to the contact form, so booking a call means submitting that
form, sending an email, or calling.

Cadre does not publish a response time for enquiries.
