# Services

## What Cadre does

Cadre AI is a consultancy focused on using AI to drive real revenue growth and improve
EBITDA. Many companies get less efficient as they scale; Cadre helps them scale with less
overhead by identifying the right AI strategy rather than throwing tools at the problem.

Cadre acts as an integrated AI team — AI strategists, AI managers and AI engineers — working
through an eight-pillar framework, and describes its own method as "Find. Prepare. Implement."

There are four core services: AI Strategy, AI Leadership & Facilitation, AI Engineering, and
AI Agents.

The positioning on cadreai.com is "From AI Confusion to AI Confidence": Cadre helps a company
pinpoint the right AI opportunities, implement them, and deliver business impact. The three
outcomes it names are Drive Revenue, Increase Profitability, and Elevate Employees.

## AI Strategy

The AI Transformation Intensive takes a company from zero clarity to a prioritised roadmap in
45 days. The engagement model has four steps: Discover Use Cases, Survey the Landscape,
Implement Solutions, and Scale with Confidence (expand into more departments, establish
champions, and set a three-year roadmap).

## The AI Transformation Intensive

The Intensive runs for 45 days. Cadre describes it as "not about tools, not about pilots" but
"the full, co-authored blueprint for how your organization will evolve with AI now and over
the next 3 years."

It runs in six steps: kickoff; the AI Maturity Index; a full-day workshop that uses the
Maturity Index findings; a prioritised use-case library (immediate, future, not prioritised);
a three-year vision; and a twelve-month roadmap designed to raise the organisation's AI
Maturity Index score over time.

Cadre publishes no price, no dates and no location for the Intensive. Starting one means
contacting the team.

## AI Leadership and Facilitation

Workshops and intensives for leadership teams, offered as a two-day Leadership Intensive, a
one-day workshop, a half-day executive session, or a one-hour virtual kickoff.

Sessions are 30% teaching, 30% interaction and 40% application to the team's real business
challenges, and the facilitation is backed by behavioural science. The teaching covers the
eight-pillar framework, how to spot AI-ready use cases, and how to message AI adoption to
teams who are sceptical or afraid. Teams leave able to spot AI-ready use cases, address fear
and uncertainty, build alignment, and act on a plan.

## AI Engineering

Connecting systems, automating workflows and adding AI intelligence: automating repetitive
work, connecting disconnected systems, adding AI to existing tools, and deploying custom
agents. The process is to understand the problem, pick the right approach, then integrate and
automate across the tech stack's APIs.

Cadre does not build by default. Where a tool a company already uses has AI features, Cadre
helps activate them; where a good off-the-shelf tool exists, Cadre recommends it; custom
solutions are built only when there is no faster or smarter option.

## AI Agents

Custom AI agents for every team and workflow, in three tiers: prompts and assistants; voice
agents for intake, qualification, support and internal routing; and fully fledged AI agents
that plan, take actions across tools and run end-to-end processes with guardrails and human
oversight.

Cadre publishes a filterable catalogue of agent examples, including a Change Order Tracker, a
Quote Approval Router, a Team Check-in Facilitator, a Training Module Builder, an Escalation
Decision Advisor, a Slack IT Supporter, a Sales Objection Coach, an Investment Committee Prep
agent, an Automated Takeoff Generator and an Invoice Query Resolver.

## How an engagement works

Cadre works with a company's current tools and systems. An engagement starts with a deep
review of the existing tech stack and processes, then identifies where AI can plug in — from
simple workflow automation to agents that connect several tools for automated execution.

The sequence Cadre describes for a private-equity client is: score the company on the AI
Maturity Index; choose the LLM, comparing options such as Microsoft Copilot, OpenAI and
Claude feature by feature; run a departmental deep dive over about 30 days; produce a
catalogue of solutions with time and ROI estimates; prioritise by revenue, profitability and
employee elevation; then implement. Departmental deep dives assess each team's people,
processes and technology.
