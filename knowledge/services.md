# Services

## What Cadre does

Cadre AI is a consultancy focused on using AI to drive real revenue growth and improve
EBITDA. Many companies get less efficient as they scale; Cadre helps them scale with less
overhead by identifying the right AI strategy rather than throwing tools at the problem.

Cadre acts as an integrated AI team — AI strategists, AI managers and AI engineers — working
through an eight-pillar framework, and describes its own method as "Find. Prepare. Implement."

There are four core services: AI Strategy, AI Leadership & Facilitation, AI Engineering, and
AI Agents.

## AI Strategy

The AI Transformation Intensive takes a company from zero clarity to a prioritised roadmap in
45 days. The engagement model has four steps: Discover Use Cases, Survey the Landscape,
Implement Solutions, and Scale with Confidence (expand into more departments, establish
champions, and set a three-year roadmap).

The Intensive itself runs in six steps: kickoff; the AI Maturity Index; a full-day workshop;
a prioritised use-case library (immediate, future, not prioritised); a three-year vision; and
a twelve-month roadmap.

## AI Leadership and Facilitation

Workshops and intensives for leadership teams, offered as a two-day Leadership Intensive, a
one-day workshop, a half-day executive session, or a one-hour virtual kickoff.

Sessions are 30% teaching, 30% interaction and 40% application to the team's real business
challenges, and the facilitation is backed by behavioural science. Teams leave able to spot
AI-ready use cases, address fear and uncertainty, build alignment, and act on a plan.

## AI Engineering

Connecting systems, automating workflows and adding AI intelligence: automating repetitive
work, connecting disconnected systems, adding AI to existing tools, and deploying custom
agents. The process is to understand the problem, pick the right approach, then integrate and
automate across the tech stack's APIs.

Cadre does not build by default. Where a tool a company already uses has AI features, Cadre
helps activate them; where a good off-the-shelf tool exists, Cadre recommends it; custom
solutions are built only when there is no faster or smarter option.

## AI Agents

Custom AI agents for every team and workflow, in three tiers: prompts and assistants; voice
agents for intake, qualification, support and internal routing; and fully fledged AI agents
that plan, take actions across tools and run end-to-end processes with guardrails and human
oversight.

Cadre publishes a filterable catalogue of agent examples, including a Change Order Tracker, a
Quote Approval Router, an Escalation Decision Advisor, an Investment Committee Prep agent and
an Invoice Query Resolver.
