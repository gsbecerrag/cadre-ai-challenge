# Partners and model selection

## The partners Cadre names

Cadre AI is an Official OpenAI Service Partner, announced in January 2026: the partnership
covers helping organisations integrate ChatGPT for Business, design CustomGPTs, deploy AI
agents, and lead workforce up-skilling programmes.

The badge on cadreai.com reads "Cadre AI — Anthropic & OpenAI Partner".

The partner logo row on the site names Meta, OpenAI, Snowflake, Salesforce, Microsoft and AWS.
Google, AWS, Snowflake and Salesforce appear as logos only — no page on the site describes
what those partnerships involve, so Cadre publishes the relationship and not its detail.

## The models Cadre works with

The models named on cadreai.com are Google Gemini, Claude, OpenAI, Microsoft Copilot, Qwen,
DeepSeek, Kimi, Mistral, Grok, Meta AI, Perplexity, and OpenRouter for model access. N8N is
referenced as an automation tool.

Cadre is vendor-agnostic in stance: "sometimes we build, sometimes we don't", and it
recommends an off-the-shelf tool when a good one exists.

## How Cadre approaches LLM selection

Choosing the LLM is a named part of the work, not an afterthought. On the homepage it is
"Optimize your LLM" — Cadre helps a company "select and configure the LLM(s) that best align
with your tech stack and business goals". In the eight-pillar framework it is pillar 2, the AI
Command Center: the company-wide decision about whether to go all in on ChatGPT, on Copilot,
or on Claude, made with feature comparisons rather than preference.

## Matching the model to the task

Cadre's published position on model choice is to tier tasks by the model they need rather than
sending everything to the largest one:

- Claude Haiku for classification, routing and form extraction.
- Claude Sonnet for multi-step research and synthesis.
- Claude Opus for complex due diligence where errors carry significant consequences.

The cost gap between the smallest and largest tier "can exceed 90 percent per task". The
policy steps Cadre recommends are to define the task tiers, run an exception process, monitor
usage, and review quarterly.
