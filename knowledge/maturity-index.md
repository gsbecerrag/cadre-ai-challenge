# AI Maturity Index

## What the AI Maturity Index is

The AI Maturity Index scores a company across Cadre's eight-pillar framework for AI
transformation. Cadre's published answer is: "It scores your company across our eight-pillar
framework for AI transformation. You'll get a grade in each area with clear explanations, plus
actionable insights on how to improve and move further along in your AI journey."

It maps where an organisation stands today on its AI journey, including areas of excitement
and fear across the team, how people prefer to learn, and how AI is already being used. Cadre
also describes it as a one-to-one-hundred scale.

## Where the Index sits in an engagement

The AI Maturity Index is step 2 of the 45-day AI Transformation Intensive, after the kickoff.
Its findings feed the full-day workshop that follows, and the twelve-month roadmap at the end
of the Intensive is designed to raise the score over time.

## The eight pillars

The 8 Pillars of AI Transformation, as Cadre publishes them:

1. Build your dedicated AI team.
2. Deploy your AI Command Center.
3. Create an AI-First Culture Shift.
4. Connect & Enable your Tech Stack.
5. AI-Healthy Data Assessment.
6. Build your Framework for AI Agent Readiness.
7. Departmental AI Deep Dives.
8. Find your 3-Year AI Vision.

Pillar 2, the AI Command Center, is the company-wide LLM decision — whether to go all in on
ChatGPT, on Copilot, or on Claude. Pillar 3 starts with an AI policy. Pillar 4 covers API
access to the tech stack and unpacking the AI features already built into tools a company
owns, such as Salesforce.

## How to get scored

There is no self-serve quiz, form or scoring tool on cadreai.com. The only published route to
a score is to contact Cadre: the site's "Get Your AI Maturity Index" call to action goes to
the contact form.

So getting scored means submitting the contact form at https://www.cadreai.com/contact,
writing to hello@gocadre.ai, or calling (619) 324-3223 — and the Index is then run as part of
the engagement.
