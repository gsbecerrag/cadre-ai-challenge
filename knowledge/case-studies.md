# Case studies

## How Cadre publishes its case studies

Cadre publishes eight case studies on one page. Every client is anonymised as a
"Non-Disclosed Company", so no client can be named. Each study gives the industry, what was
built, and quantified results.

## Lead Processing Agent, Professional Services

45 hours a month saved, handling 1,500+ emails and 650+ leads a month across five branches.

## Supplier Automation, Manufacturing & Logistics

Built across Zendesk and NetSuite: 220 hours a month saved, 60% faster processing, and 90%
accuracy.

## Proposal Automation, Manufacturing & Logistics

8,000+ hours a year saved on 20–30 proposals a week; a proposal that took one to two days now
takes about 20 minutes.

## AI-Powered Housing Visibility System, Hospitality

$420,000 saved annually, against a previous spend of $35,000 a month in expedited cleaning
fees.

## AI Scheduling System, Real Estate

57% higher daily efficiency, 50% more field capacity, 72% less fuel used, and $136,000 more
revenue per field specialist.

## Email Agent Automation, Manufacturing & Logistics

3,500 hours a year saved across 55 sales representatives handling 4,000+ emails a month; nine
hours a week of email became about 55 minutes.

## Loan Intelligence Assistant, Financial Services and Mortgage & Lending

An assistant called "LIA" for 27 loan officers: 2,500 hours a year saved, a task that took one
to two days now under 15 minutes, and 3,960+ chats in the first 90 days.

## AI Voice and Chat Agents, Professional Services

1,500 hours a year saved, automating 500–700 appointment requests a month.
