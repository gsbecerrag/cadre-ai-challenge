# Data security

## What Cadre publishes about data security

Cadre's published data-security commitments appear on its AI Engineering page, and there are
three of them:

- Black-box your data so it is never used to train other models.
- Stop employees from sharing company secrets on personal LLMs.
- Get your entire team on secure, compliant AI tools.

Cadre's own framing of the problem is the one in its homepage video: some employees using
ChatGPT, others using Claude, and sensitive company information going into both.

These three statements are the whole of what Cadre publishes on the subject. Anything beyond
them — a certification, a data-processing agreement, encryption or residency detail — is not
published, and the honest answer is to say so and put the question to the team.

## Guardrails on the agents Cadre builds

Fully fledged AI agents plan, take actions across tools and run end-to-end processes "with
guardrails and human oversight". Human oversight is part of how Cadre describes the agents it
deploys, not an add-on.

## The AI policy that comes with a culture shift

Pillar 3 of the eight-pillar framework, the AI-First Culture Shift, starts with an AI policy —
so agreeing the rules for how staff may use AI is part of the engagement rather than something
a company is left to write alone.

## Cadre's own privacy policy and terms

Cadre AI is the trading name of AI Gurus LLC. Its privacy policy, last updated 25 June 2026,
says the site collects name, email, phone, IP address, cookies, usage, device and geolocation
data, with a stated retention period of two years; privacy questions go to privacy@gocadre.ai.

The terms of service, last updated 15 June 2026, state that the services are provided "AS-IS
and AS-AVAILABLE", that users are solely responsible for the data they transmit, and that
California law applies with arbitration in San Diego.

These cover Cadre's own website. They are not a description of how client data is handled
inside an engagement, which Cadre does not publish.
